Independent checker

The checks of §3.7, from an origin neither the voting application nor the wallet controls.

—

Independence is a property, not an address This page is served by the demo operator, who also runs the office and the box — so in this deployment the independence is asserted rather than demonstrated. What makes the checks worth anything is that the artefacts are self-authenticating: the released records carry their own signatures, and a denial is signed under k_s^(O). Anyone may serve this page and reach the same conclusions.

Anyone · the same check, on files published elsewhere

After the close the office, the ballot box and the administering body publish these three. Take them from an archive, a mirror, or anywhere that is not this deployment, and check them here — or better, with the program below on a machine none of these origins can reach.

Election_config_….json from the administering body
EBB_dump_….json from the ballot box
VRO_dump_….json from the office

The downloads are off until the box closes, and this page does not learn that it has by itself — press this after closing rather than reloading.

Each file is recognised by what is in it, so a file in the wrong place is named rather than discovered halfway through. VRO_supervision_….json is a different artefact — it names citizens and is not part of this check.

One voter · is my ballot still where the box said it was?

D2. The box returns a receipt for every submission, signed under its statement key k_s^(B). Load it here and two separate things are checked: that the box really said it, against the key pinned in the published configuration, and that the registry still says it, against the commitment published at that position. The first catches a box denying its own receipt; the second catches one dropping an entry it acknowledged. Neither takes the box's word for anything.
