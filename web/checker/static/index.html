Independence is a property, not an address This page is served by the demo operator, who also runs the office and the box — so in this deployment the independence is asserted rather than demonstrated. What makes the checks worth anything is that the artefacts are self-authenticating: the released records carry their own signatures, and a denial is signed under k_s^(O). Anyone may serve this page and reach the same conclusions.
