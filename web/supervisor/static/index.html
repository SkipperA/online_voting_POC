There is no login: whoever can reach this origin is a supervisor, so the control is the perimeter it is served behind, and anything taken off this page carries no protection with it.

What this cannot show you

A single well-formed release recorded for an eligible citizen who abstained and never checked is indistinguishable here from a genuine one. Separating them needs the voter's signed request, which the office verifies and does not keep — retaining what authenticates a request would turn a transient credential into a durable record of who asked. That gap is the reason the issuing power should be split across mutually independent offices.
